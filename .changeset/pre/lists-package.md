'@panneau/lists': major

Add `@panneau/lists`, which renders the list state of `@panneau/core`. `ListView` renders a list made with `useList()` and the items of its query; `List` does the same for items held in memory, from columns and items, with or without a definition. Lists have columns with displays (a registry entry per display, extended by plugins), table and cards layouts that turn into stacked rows on phones, sorting, search, filter controls with chips for the active filters, pagination, selection of ids or of every matching item with exceptions, and row and toolbar actions with confirmations, hidden when the user or the item doesn't allow them. Messages are in English and French, in `@panneau/intl`.
