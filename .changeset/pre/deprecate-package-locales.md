The messages of `@panneau/ui`, `@panneau/fields` and `@panneau/forms` now live in `@panneau/intl`. `@panneau/ui/locales`, `@panneau/fields/locales` and `@panneau/forms/locales` still export the `en` and `fr` messages of their package, but are deprecated: use `withPanneauMessages()`, `en` or `fr` from `@panneau/intl`, which have the messages of every package. `fieldsPlugin` now carries the French messages of every package, those of `@panneau/forms` included.
