Densities (decision 0024, step 6). `PanneauRoot` takes `density`: `compact`, `comfortable`, `touch`, or `auto` (the default, as before: touch on touch screens). New density tokens `--panneau-control-padding-x` and `--panneau-row-padding-y`. The paddings of buttons, inputs, list box options and list tables now follow the density. Themes redefine each density in `densities`, and `bootstrapTheme` sets Bootstrap's control sizes for the comfortable density only. `@panneau/protocol` adds `densities` to the `Theme` schema and `density` to the definition's theme. Storybook has a density switcher.
