Global element rules of a site, such as Bootstrap's Reboot, no longer leak into Panneau: a `h5` card title no longer gets `margin-bottom: 0.5rem`, legends no longer float, and dividers are no longer faded. The reset of the root now weighs two element selectors, more than a site's element rules and less than any class, and components reset the elements they render (titles, legends, paragraphs, lists, rules) in their own classes.
