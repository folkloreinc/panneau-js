Going back to a list from an item's form shows it as the user left it. The link above the title, the breadcrumbs and the page after a delete now open the list's last URL in the tab, with its filters, sort, page or cursor, search and layout, rather than the bare list with its remembered view. The menus and a create still open the bare list. The browser's Back and Forward restore the scroll of the page they go to; any other navigation still starts at the top and moves the focus to the title (decision 0102).
