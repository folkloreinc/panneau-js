The details of an item show its values in full, as list cells don't. Displays get a `variant` (`DisplayVariant`): `cell` in lists, where nothing changes, and `details` in `ItemDetails`, which plugins' displays get too. In details, related items are a list linked to their items, through the `getItemHref()` of the relation source of the fields (the app gives it), with their image, their description and the labels of plain ids; every media shows, not the first one; rich text keeps its formatting, sanitized by DOMPurify (a new dependency of `@panneau/lists`, loaded with the first rich text shown in details, and plain text until then: HTML only, without scripts, event handlers, `style` and `class` attributes, `<style>` blocks or form controls, links to `http`, `https`, `mailto`, `tel` and relative URLs only, and `rel="noopener noreferrer"` on those that open a new tab); text keeps its line breaks and JSON is indented, without the two-line clamp of a cell; and a localized value shows each of its locales under its name, the interface's locale first.
