Typography roles and fonts in themes (decision 0024, step 4). `@panneau/ui` adds the font roles `--panneau-font-family-body`, `-heading` and `-display` (`--panneau-font-family` is now a deprecated alias of the body family), `--panneau-font-numeric`, the `Heading` component with its size tokens (1 to 4, `display-1`, `display-2`), `Text`, and an opt-in `Prose` container for rich content. Themes take `fonts`, font faces or hosted stylesheets, which `PanneauRoot` adds to the page once. `bootstrapTheme` now sets the body family. `@panneau/protocol` adds `fonts` to the `Theme` schema. Numbers in fields and lists use `--panneau-font-numeric`.
