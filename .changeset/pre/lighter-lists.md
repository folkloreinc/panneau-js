Lighter lists (decision 0068): in the table, the arrow of a sortable column shows on hover and focus, and always on the sorted column; the checkbox of a row shows on hover and focus, and on every row once one is selected; both always show on touch screens. Headers lose their background, rows get one on hover, and an item without an image shows an empty square instead of an icon. In the gallery, the checkbox of an item moves from its image to its caption, and a selected item has an accent outline.
