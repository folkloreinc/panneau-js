Relation fields show the labels of values that are only ids (`"12"`), and can create items from the picker. `RelationSource` has two optional methods: `resolve(resource, ids)`, which gives the references of ids (fields that show the same ids share one request), and `create(resource, text)`, with `canCreate(resource)`, which the picker offers as "Create “…”". `createRelationSource()` resolves ids with the adapter's `get()`, and creates items with its `create()` when given `create: true` (the text becomes the `itemTitle`, in every locale when it is localized) or a function that gives the values. `RelationField` takes `onCreate`, which can open a dialog and resolves with the new reference. `useRelationReferences()` resolves references for other components. In `@panneau/lists`, relation filters resolve the labels of ids that come from the URL, for their chips and picker. `@panneau/intl` has the new messages, in English and French.
