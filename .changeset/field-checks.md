Fields check their value with the backend, and the slug field does what sites' `PageSlug` did (decision 0032). A field with `check: true` sends `POST /resources/{resource}/fields/{field}/check` as it is typed (`FieldCheckRequest`), and the backend answers whether the value is available, a free suggestion, the URL's `prefix` and `url`, and `disabled` for an item the field doesn't apply to (`FieldCheckResponse`). The mock backend answers it, and the conformance kit checks it (`fields.check`). `DataAdapter.checkField?()` sends it; `createFieldCheckSource(adapter, resource, id)` gives the fields context its `checkSource`, which `FormView` takes too. The `slug` field follows its source in its own locale, or in the default one, shows the backend's URL, and offers the suggestion when the slug is taken. Field entries get the `locale` of the value they edit.
