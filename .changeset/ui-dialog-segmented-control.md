`Dialog` gets an `xl` size (`--panneau-dialog-width-xl`, 72rem) for pickers and editors, `fullScreenOnPhone` to take the whole screen on phones instead of opening as a sheet at the bottom, and `headerContent`, shown between the title and the close button (under the title on phones). New `SegmentedControl`: a row of a few choices, one always selected, as a radio group moved through with the arrow keys, with icons, two sizes, `fullWidth`, and its component tokens (`--panneau-segmented-control-*`).
