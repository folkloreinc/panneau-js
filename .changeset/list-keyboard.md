Lists take the keyboard, on the registry of shortcuts (decision 0112), listed in the "?" help: `j`/`k` and the arrows move between the rows of the table and the cards, Enter opens the focused item, `x` selects it, Shift with a move or a click on a checkbox extends the selection, Esc clears it, `/` goes to the search. On the page of a resource, `c` does what "New …" does.
