The checkboxes of a table stay in view when it scrolls sideways, the one of the page in the header: a table wider than its list, such as next to an item's panel, no longer hides them once a link at the end of a row takes the focus. `delete` alone in the menu of a row is now an icon button at the end of the row, with the item in its accessible name and the same confirmation; with other actions, it stays in the menu (decision 0060).
