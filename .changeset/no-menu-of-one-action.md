A menu never holds a single action (decision 0088, amended). In the header of an item, a single action that destroys, such as "Delete", is a secondary button after the others, not the only item of a "More actions" menu, nor a solid danger button; two or more go in the menu. In rows, a single action that destroys is its danger icon again, last; two or more go in the row's menu, at every width.
